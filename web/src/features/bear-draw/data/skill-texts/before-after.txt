# 前后对比

> 本文件由小熊API整理。「最佳实践原文」里的英文段落是开源项目的原文摘录，每段上方标了来源和许可证；其余部分是我们写的。许可证全文见 `../LICENSES/`，致谢清单见 `../../CREDITS.md`。

## 适用

- 收纳、清洁、家居改造、修图服务、装修、软件界面这类「看得见」的变化，并且用户有真实的前后照片。
- 新旧款对比、和普通做法对比（只比能证明的差别）。
- 不适用（要先停下来说明）：化妆品、护肤、祛痘、美白、减肥、生发、保健食品、医疗器械、医美。这些类目的效果对比必须有真实依据，不能用 AI 生成「使用后」的样子；可以改做使用步骤图、成分说明图或真实买家秀排版。

## 工作流程

1. **先判断类目**：属于上面「不适用」的类目，就告诉用户原因，并给出可做的替代方案，不出效果对比。
2. **先问缺的**（最多 3 个问题）
   - 有没有真实的「前」和「后」照片（同一角度、同一光线最好）？
   - 对比放在哪里：主图 / 详情页 / 广告 / 小红书。
   - 两边的标签怎么写（用顾客自己的话，如「抽屉乱成一团」→「拉开就找到」）。
3. **列交付清单**，确认后出图：

   | # | 名称 | 比例 | 交付尺寸 | 模型 | 要点 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | 左右对比 | 1:1 | 800×800 | gpt-image-2.5-sunburst | Image 1 = 前，Image 2 = 后，中间分隔线 + 箭头 |
   | 2 | 上下对比 | 3:4 | 750×1000 | gpt-image-2.5-sunburst | 上前下后，适合手机竖屏 |
   | 3 | 详情页对比屏（可选） | 2:3 | 750×1125 | gpt-image-2.5-sunburst | 标题 + 对比 + 一句说明 |

4. **一张图调用一次**（`edit_image`，带两张真实照片）。提示词写清「只做排版、统一色温和裁切，不改照片里的内容」。只有一张「前」的照片、用户又要展示改造思路时，必须在图上标「效果示意」。
5. **检查**：两边是不是同一个场景、同一角度；有没有偷偷美化「后」或丑化「前」；标签是否属实；跑 `data/ad-law-words.json`。

### 前后对比模板与合规提醒

> 来源 / Source: coreyhaines31/marketingskills · skills/ad-creative/references/static-ad-templates.md › Template 6 Before / After · MIT (verbatim)

````text
### 6. Before / After

Split image with arrow between. Transformation framing — product results, workflow, or visual proof.

- **Tier**: B — **Role**: mid-funnel supporting cast. Before/afters (and their cousin, progression ads) convert well for people already problem-aware; they show the payoff but rarely open cold reach on their own.
- **Structure**: Two panels, arrow or divider, minimal copy labeling each state
- **Copy slot**: Label the states in the customer's words ("Sunday-night spreadsheet dread" → "Reports send themselves")
- **DTC example**: Skin, energy, space — the classic visual transformation
- **SaaS example**: Cluttered 6-tab workflow → one clean dashboard
- **Compliance note**: Before/after claims are regulated in health, finance, and beauty — verify platform policy before using
- **Source it from**: Transformation language in reviews ("I used to X, now I Y")
````

> 来源 / Source: coreyhaines31/marketingskills · skills/ad-creative/references/static-ad-templates.md › Template 2 Us vs. Them · MIT (verbatim)

````text
### 2. Us vs. Them

Side-by-side comparison. Competitor or "old way" on the left (grayed out), your product on the right (full color). 4-6 comparison rows.

- **Tier**: B — **Role**: mid-funnel supporting cast. "Us vs. them" reliably sneaks into a brand's top 8; converts well for people already weighing you against an alternative, but rarely the format that opens cold net-new reach.
- **Structure**: Two columns, check/cross marks per row, your side visually alive
- **Copy slot**: Comparison rows — each row a real differentiator, not filler
- **DTC example**: "Their multivitamin: 13 ingredients. Ours: 60."
- **SaaS example**: "Spreadsheets: 6 hours a week. Us: 6 minutes."
- **Source it from**: Reviews that mention switching, or comments comparing you to a competitor
````

### 不编造

> 来源 / Source: coreyhaines31/marketingskills · skills/ad-creative/references/static-ad-templates.md › Generation Rules · MIT (verbatim)

````text
## Generation Rules

- Every variation must include: **template name, headline copy, body copy, visual description, source grounding**
- Source grounding = which review, winning ad, or comment this concept is based on
- Never produce a variation without source grounding — no invented claims, stats, or testimonials
- Pull copy directly from customer language whenever possible; don't paraphrase reviews into marketing-speak
- Match the brand voice doc on tone, not generic direct-response voice
- Real names, real stats, real quotes only — fabricated social proof is a compliance and trust violation
````

> 来源 / Source: coreyhaines31/marketingskills · skills/ad-creative/SKILL.md › Grounded Inputs · MIT (verbatim)

````text
**Grounding rules:**
- Every concept cites its source (which review, winning ad, or comment it traces to)
- No invented claims, stats, or testimonials — ever
- If `inputs/winning-ads/` or `inputs/reviews/` is empty, stop and ask the user to populate it before generating. Do not generate ungrounded concepts as a fallback.
- Inputs decay: refresh `inputs/winning-ads/` as new ads scale; refresh `inputs/reviews/` and `inputs/comments/` monthly
````

## 本站调整

- **原文的合规提醒升级成硬规则**：marketingskills 原文写「健康、金融、美容类的前后对比受监管，用前查平台政策」。我们直接规定：化妆品、保健、医疗、减肥、生发、医美类不做 AI 效果对比；其他类目优先用真实照片。
- **依据从哪来**：原文要求每个概念引用 `inputs/` 里的真实评价或素材。本站没有这个文件夹，依据就是用户上传的真实前后照片和用户提供的顾客原话。
- **真实照片优先**：对比图默认是「排版两张真实照片」，不是「生成两张图」。模型只负责拼版、统一色温、加分隔线和标签，不改照片内容。
- **示意要标出来**：用户只有「前」的照片、想展示改造方向时，可以用编辑生成一张方案图，但图上必须写「效果示意」，交付说明里也写清。不能把示意图当作真实效果宣传。
- **竞品对比**：原文 Us vs. Them 模板把「对方」做成灰色。我们不点名真实竞品，用「普通款」「传统做法」代替，只比能证明的差别。
- **广告法**：标签里不写「100% 去除」「彻底根除」「永久」「立竿见影」等；数字要有出处。详见 `data/ad-law-words.json`。
- **模型**：拼版和精修用 gpt-image-2.5-sunburst；只做方案示意草图时可用 gpt-image-2。
- **尺寸**：本站出图通常是 1024×1024 或 1024×1536，比例写进提示词，页面再缩放到交付尺寸。
- **导出**：ZIP 里按 `对比-左右-1x1.jpg`、`对比-上下-3x4.jpg` 命名。
- **示例**：原文的房间换椅子只是示意；我们的示例用虚构品牌「方寸收纳」抽屉分隔盒。

### 示例（虚构品牌「方寸收纳」，左右对比）

```text
Use case: compositing
Asset type: before/after comparison, square 1:1
Input images: Image 1: real photo of a messy kitchen drawer (before); Image 2: real photo of the same drawer with FANGCUN organizer trays (after)
Primary request: place Image 1 on the left half and Image 2 on the right half with a thin white divider and a small arrow
Composition/framing: square 1:1; both photos cropped to the same drawer edges and the same height; labels in a top band
Lighting/mood: match the color temperature of both photos to neutral daylight
Text (verbatim): left label "之前：找把勺子翻半天"; right label "之后：一拉开就看到"
Constraints: do not change the contents of either photo; do not tidy the left photo or add items to the right photo; render each label exactly once; no other text; no watermark
Avoid: beautifying filters on only one side, sparkles, exaggerated arrows
```
