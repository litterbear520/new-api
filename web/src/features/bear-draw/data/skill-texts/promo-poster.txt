# 促销海报

> 本文件由小熊API整理。「最佳实践原文」里的英文段落是开源项目的原文摘录，每段上方标了来源和许可证；其余部分是我们写的。许可证全文见 `../LICENSES/`，致谢清单见 `../../CREDITS.md`。

## 适用

- 双11、618、年货节、中秋、店庆等活动的主海报和多尺寸延展。
- 海报上要有价格、折扣、满减、活动时间这类准确文字。
- 不适用：一组投放用的多角度广告（用 `ad-creative`）；直播间贴片（用 `live-douyin`）。

## 工作流程

1. **先问缺的**（最多 3 个问题）
   - 活动名称、时间和真实的优惠内容（如「满300减40」「到手价 ¥199」），一个字都不能猜。
   - 主推商品（上传实拍图更好）。
   - 投放位置：店铺首页 / 朋友圈 / 社群 / 门店屏，决定要哪些尺寸。
2. **先定视觉方向**：照下面原文的两步法，但只写一句方向名 + 3–4 行说明（色彩、版式、材质、字体气质），从原文的 22 种风格里挑一种做底。给用户看一眼再出图。
3. **列交付清单**，确认后出图：

   | # | 名称 | 比例 | 交付尺寸 | 模型 | 要点 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | 主海报 | 3:4 | 1080×1440 | gpt-image-2.5-sunburst | 三段式：上活动名，中商品，下优惠和时间 |
   | 2 | 竖版 | 9:16 | 1080×1920 | gpt-image-2.5-sunburst | 用主海报做 Image 1 编辑延展，文字不改 |
   | 3 | 横版 Banner | 16:9 | 1920×1080 | gpt-image-2.5-sunburst | 同上，商品放右侧，文字放左侧 |
   | 4 | 方图（可选） | 1:1 | 1080×1080 | gpt-image-2.5-sunburst | 朋友圈、社群用 |

4. **一张图调用一次**：先出主海报，用户满意后再用 `edit_image` 延展到其他尺寸，提示词写「只扩展背景、调整排版，商品和所有文字不变」。
5. **检查**：价格、折扣、日期逐字核对；数字和单位不能错一个；跑 `data/ad-law-words.json`；文字不贴边、不重叠。

**"Analog Meditation"**
Philosophy: Quiet visual contemplation through texture and breathing room.
Visual expression: Paper grain, ink bleeds, vast negative space. Photography and illustration dominate. Typography whispered (small, restrained, serving the visual). Japanese photobook aesthetic. Images breathe across pages. Text appears sparingly - short phrases, never explanatory blocks. Each composition balanced with the care of a meditation practice.

**"Organic Systems"**
Philosophy: Natural clustering and modular growth patterns.
Visual expression: Rounded forms, organic arrangements, color from nature through architecture. Information shown through visual diagrams, spatial relationships, iconography. Text only for key labels floating in space. The composition tells the story through expert spatial orchestration.

**"Geometric Silence"**
Philosophy: Pure order and restraint.
Visual expression: Grid-based precision, bold photography or stark graphics, dramatic negative space. Typography precise but minimal - small essential text, large quiet zones. Swiss formalism meets Brutalist material honesty. Structure communicates, not words. Every alignment the work of countless refinements.

## 22 Art Direction Styles

````text
## Design Principles

### Visual Hierarchy (3-Zone Rule)
- **Top**: Logo or main value prop
- **Middle**: Supporting message + visuals
- **Bottom**: CTA (button/QR/URL)

### Safe Zones
- Critical content in central 70-80% of canvas
- Avoid text/CTA within 50-100px of edges
- YouTube: 1546 × 423px safe area inside 2560 × 1440
- Meta/Instagram: central 80% to avoid UI chrome

### CTA Rules
- One CTA per banner
- High contrast vs background
- Bottom-right placement (terminal area)
- Min 44px height for mobile tap targets
- Action verbs: "Get", "Start", "Download", "Claim"

### Typography
- Max 2 typefaces per banner
- Min 16px body, ≥32px headline (digital)
- Min 4.5:1 contrast ratio
- Max 7 words/line, 3 lines for ads

### Text-to-Image Ratio
- Ads: under 20% text (Meta penalizes)
- Social covers: 60/40 image-to-text
- Print: 70pt+ headlines for 3-5m viewing distance
````

**Text as a contextual element**: Text is always minimal and visual-first, but let context guide whether that means whisper-quiet labels or bold typographic gestures. A punk venue poster might have larger, more aggressive type than a minimalist ceramics studio identity. Most of the time, font should be thin. All use of fonts must be design-forward and prioritize visual communication. Regardless of text scale, nothing falls off the page and nothing overlaps. ## 本站调整

- **方向说明缩短**：Anthropic 原文要求写 4–6 段「设计哲学」，再画成 90% 视觉、10% 文字的艺术作品。促销海报要卖货，文字比例更高，所以我们只写一句方向名 + 3–4 行说明，用来锁定整套的色彩和气质。原文「不加东西、只打磨」的收尾要求照用。
- **文字准确第一**：价格、折扣、日期全部放进 Text (verbatim)，并写「render exactly once」。原文 Cookbook 的节日卡写法（Include ONLY this text）照用。
- **价格合规**：只写商家真实的优惠；写「原价」「划线价」要有依据（促销前 7 天内的最低成交价），没有依据就只写到手价；满减、券要写清门槛。
- **广告法**：不写「全网最低」「史上最低」「最划算」「第一」「仅此一次」（除非属实）等，详见 `data/ad-law-words.json`。
- **节日元素**：中秋、春节可以用月亮、灯笼、祥云等传统元素，不用别家 IP 角色、明星形象和其他品牌 Logo。
- **多尺寸延展**：先出 3:4 主海报，再用编辑延展，保证文字和商品一致；原文的「CTA 放右下」在横版里照用，竖版放底部安全区。
- **模型**：海报都带字，默认 gpt-image-2.5-sunburst；只出无字背景时可用 gpt-image-2。
- **尺寸**：本站出图通常是 1024×1536 或 1536×1024，页面再裁到 3:4、9:16、16:9，所以主体和字离边至少留 8%。
- **导出**：ZIP 里按 `海报-主图-3x4.jpg`、`海报-竖版-9x16.jpg`、`海报-横版-16x9.jpg` 命名。
- **示例**：原文的圣诞卡、洗发水广告牌只是示意；我们的示例用虚构品牌「栖木」实木家具。

### 示例（虚构品牌「栖木」，双11 主海报）

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: Double 11 sale poster, portrait 3:4
Primary request: warm, premium sale poster for a solid-wood side table
Input images: Image 1: product photo of the QIMU walnut side table (keep exactly)
Scene/backdrop: minimal living-room corner, warm plaster wall, late-afternoon sun shapes on the wall
Subject: the side table with a ceramic vase and an open book, product fully visible
Style/medium: editorial interior photography with bold modern typography; direction "Quiet Grain": warm neutrals, generous space, one strong accent
Composition/framing: portrait 3:4; top band event title; middle product; bottom band offer and date; 8% safe margin all around
Lighting/mood: golden hour, calm and premium
Color palette: walnut brown, linen white #F4EEE6, accent persimmon #D9532B
Text (verbatim): "栖木 双11" (top, large); "满300减40" (bottom, largest); "11.1–11.11" (bottom, small)
Constraints: preserve the table exactly as in Image 1; render each text exactly once; no other text; no other logos; no watermark
Avoid: crossed-out prices, starbursts with extra words, clutter around the product
```
