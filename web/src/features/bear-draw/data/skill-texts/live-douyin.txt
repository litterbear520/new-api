# 直播间与抖音素材

> 本文件由小熊API整理。「最佳实践原文」里的英文段落是开源项目的原文摘录，每段上方标了来源和许可证；其余部分是我们写的。许可证全文见 `../LICENSES/`，致谢清单见 `../../CREDITS.md`。

## 适用

- 抖音、视频号、快手直播：绿幕背景图、价格贴片、福利贴片、预告海报。
- 短视频封面：9:16 竖版，标题放在安全区内。
- 不适用：小红书 3:4 图文（用 `xhs-cover`）；电商主图（用 `taobao-main-set`）。

## 工作流程

1. **先问缺的**（最多 3 个问题）
   - 直播主题和时间（预告海报上的时间必须是真的）。
   - 主推商品和真实的价格/福利（没有就不放价格）。
   - 主播站位：居中 / 偏左 / 偏右，桌面上放不放商品。
2. **列交付清单**，确认后出图：

   | # | 名称 | 比例 | 交付尺寸 | 模型 | 要点 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | 直播间背景 | 9:16 | 1080×1920 | gpt-image-2 | 中间 60% 留给主播，主题字放上方安全区，不放小字 |
   | 2 | 价格贴片 | 1:1 | 600×600 | gpt-image-2.5-flare | 透明底 PNG，价格和福利逐字 |
   | 3 | 福利贴片 | 3:2 | 900×600 | gpt-image-2.5-flare | 透明底 PNG，如「关注领券」 |
   | 4 | 直播预告海报 | 3:4 | 1080×1440 | gpt-image-2.5-sunburst | 主题 + 时间 + 主推商品 |
   | 5 | 视频封面 | 9:16 | 1080×1920 | gpt-image-2.5-sunburst | 标题在中间安全带，四边不放关键内容 |

3. **一张图一段提示词，一张图调用一次**：整套共用风格锁（主题色、字体风格、装饰元素）。贴片用 `generate_image` 并带 `background=transparent`、`output_format=png`。
4. **检查**：主播区是否干净；所有字在安全区内；价格、时间与用户提供的一致；跑 `data/ad-law-words.json`；画面里不放二维码、微信号、站外链接。

## 最佳实践原文

### 竖屏安全区

> 来源 / Source: coreyhaines31/marketingskills · skills/ad-creative/references/short-form-video-specs.md › Vertical video production spec · MIT (verbatim)

````text
### Safe zones (the single most-missed constraint)
Platform UI covers the frame edges — the action rail, caption stack, music button, and account row all sit *on top of* your video. Text or key visuals in those bands get covered. Keep everything inside the **cross-platform safe band** — the worst case of TikTok and IG Reels margins on a 1080×1920 canvas:

| Edge | Keep clear | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Top** | 220px | TikTok tabs + IG account row |
| **Bottom** | 500px | Caption / music / CTA stack (both platforms) |
| **Left** | 180px | Symmetry with right |
| **Right** | 180px | Action rail (like/comment/share/music) |

**Result: a 720×1200 centered text band, from y=220 to y=1420.** Compose all captions and load-bearing visuals inside it. Preview against a safe-zone overlay before a big push. (These numbers drift with app updates — re-verify occasionally; they're a well-sourced worst-case, not a permanent law.)
````

> 来源 / Source: nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill · .claude/skills/design/references/banner-sizes-and-styles.md › Design Principles · MIT (verbatim)

````text
## Design Principles

### Visual Hierarchy (3-Zone Rule)
- **Top**: Logo or main value prop
- **Middle**: Supporting message + visuals
- **Bottom**: CTA (button/QR/URL)

### Safe Zones
- Critical content in central 70-80% of canvas
- Avoid text/CTA within 50-100px of edges
- YouTube: 1546 × 423px safe area inside 2560 × 1440
- Meta/Instagram: central 80% to avoid UI chrome

### CTA Rules
- One CTA per banner
- High contrast vs background
- Bottom-right placement (terminal area)
- Min 44px height for mobile tap targets
- Action verbs: "Get", "Start", "Download", "Claim"

### Typography
- Max 2 typefaces per banner
- Min 16px body, ≥32px headline (digital)
- Min 4.5:1 contrast ratio
- Max 7 words/line, 3 lines for ads

### Text-to-Image Ratio
- Ads: under 20% text (Meta penalizes)
- Social covers: 60/40 image-to-text
- Print: 70pt+ headlines for 3-5m viewing distance
````

### 透明底贴片

> 来源 / Source: openai/openai-cookbook · examples/multimodal/image-gen-models-prompting-guide.ipynb › 1.1 Model parameters · MIT (verbatim)

````text
### Transparent backgrounds with `gpt-image-2` (preview)

Transparent backgrounds are available in preview for `gpt-image-2`. To generate or edit an image with a transparent background:

- Set `background="transparent"`.
- Set `output_format="png"` (the default) or `output_format="webp"`. Both support transparency; `jpeg` does not.
- Omit `output_compression` for PNG output. WebP supports optional compression.
- Explicitly request an isolated subject on a fully transparent background, with no scenery, solid backdrop, checkerboard, or unwanted shadows.
- For edits, explicitly preserve the transparent background in each prompt so later steps do not introduce a new background.
- Keep the returned image as a PNG or WebP file and preserve its alpha channel throughout downstream processing.
````

### 22. Ugly / Handwriting / Post-it

Deliberately low-polish — handwritten note, sticky note, or plain-text-on-a-photo. The anti-designed look reads native and urgent.

## 本站调整

- **安全区**：原文的 720×1200 安全带是 TikTok 和 IG Reels 的最坏情况。抖音界面和 TikTok 相近，我们先按这个数用，平台改版后要再核对（`data/platform-specs.json` 里标了 `verify`）。直播间背景另外要避开底部商品卡和评论区，主题字放在上方 220–600px 的区域。
- **直播间背景不放小字**：原文的网页背景示例要求完全无字；直播背景我们允许上方一行主题短句（≤8 字）和品牌名，其余不放字。直播画面会被压缩，小字看不清。
- **CTA 位置**：原文建议 CTA 放右下角。竖屏直播和视频的右侧、底部会被按钮和评论挡住，所以按钮类文字放在安全带内偏下的位置。
- **贴片尺寸**：600×600、900×600 是我们给的默认值，实际大小在直播伴侣里拖拽调整；贴片一定要透明底 PNG。
- **价格和时间**：只写用户给的真实价格、真实福利、真实开播时间。写「原价」要有依据（促销前 7 天内的最低成交价），没有就不写划线价。
- **抖音合规**：不写「全网最低」「最便宜」「第一」，不做「点击领奖」这类诱导字样，不放二维码和站外联系方式。详见 `data/ad-law-words.json`。
- **原文的 Ugly / Post-it 模板**：原文说它在大促期间效果好。直播福利贴片可以借这种手写便签感，但价格必须真实。
- **模型**：背景用 gpt-image-2；贴片用 gpt-image-2.5-flare（透明底）；带字的预告海报和封面用 gpt-image-2.5-sunburst。
- **尺寸**：本站出图通常是 1024×1536 竖图，9:16 由页面左右裁掉约 8%，所以主体和字离左右边至少留 10%。
- **导出**：ZIP 里按 `抖音-直播背景.jpg`、`抖音-贴片-价格.png`、`抖音-预告.jpg`、`抖音-封面.jpg` 命名。
- **示例**：原文的广告牌只是示意；我们的示例用虚构品牌「果仁社」混合坚果。

### 示例（虚构品牌「果仁社」，直播间背景）

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Douyin live-room background (green-screen replacement), portrait 9:16
Primary request: warm autumn harvest stage backdrop for a nut snacks live sale
Scene/backdrop: soft-focus wooden pantry shelves with glass jars of mixed nuts, warm string lights, gentle bokeh
Subject: no people; the center 60% of the frame is a calm, low-detail area where the host will stand
Style/medium: photorealistic set photography, shallow depth of field
Composition/framing: portrait 9:16; brand title area in the upper band (y 220–600 px of 1920); lower third darker and plain for product cards and comments; nothing important within 10% of the left and right edges
Lighting/mood: warm tungsten glow, cozy and inviting
Color palette: walnut brown, honey gold #E3A33B, cream
Text (verbatim): "果仁社 秋日囤货场" in bold rounded sans-serif, upper band only
Constraints: render the text exactly once; no prices; no QR codes; no other logos; no watermark
Avoid: busy patterns behind the host area, small text, people or hands
```
